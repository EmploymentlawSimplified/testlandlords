About The New Start Project

A new start, not a return to the old life

The New Start Project is a community interest company that works with people in recovery who are between three months and two years clean. Our focus is long-term abstinence from drugs and alcohol.

A lot of people at that stage don't know where to turn for support. They don't want to go back to the neighbourhood they came from, because if they go back to the same life and the same people, the cycle just repeats. They want to build a life of their own and live independently.

That's what we're about: giving them a new start, with a safe shared home, the chance to learn and grow, and the support to move on to independence. Our approach is simple: ordinary shared houses, not hostels, where people can live together, stay clean and work on their own plan until they're ready for a place of their own.

We're looking for landlords in South Devon willing to let us three- or four-bedroom houses. We'll take a long-term lease and pay the rent direct, every month.

If you have a three- or four-bedroom house in South Devon, call us on 01803 446133 or contact us below.

Common Questions

Landlord FAQs

  • Who will live there? Adults who are between three months and two years clean, committed to long-term abstinence and working towards independent living, with support from our team.
  • Who is my tenant? The New Start Project Community CIC (company no. 17399292), not individual residents.
  • Who deals with repairs? This is set out in the lease. We report issues promptly and carry out regular inspections.
  • Do I need my lender's or insurer's consent? Usually yes. Please check your mortgage and insurance terms before leasing.
