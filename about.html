About Us

Everyone deserves a chance to start again

The New Start Project was founded on one belief: that safe housing, delivered with genuine care, changes what's possible for a person.

What We Believe

You Are Not a Case Number

Too many supported housing providers treat people as entries on a spreadsheet. You'll be treated as a person with your own story, your own difficulties, and your own way forward.

👥

You First, Always

There's no one-size-fits-all here. We listen first, then respond to what you actually need — not what a template says someone in your situation should need. Your support plan is written with you, not about you.

🏠

Homes, Not Hostels

You should never feel watched, monitored or managed. You're an adult living in your own home. Support is there when you want it and when you need it — not imposed on you because a policy says so.

🌱

Independence Is the Point

Success isn't you staying with us. It's you managing your own tenancy and getting on with your life. The whole service exists to get you to the point where you don't need it any more.

📞

We Don't Disappear

When you move out, you can still ring us. The first weeks living alone are the hardest, and that's exactly when most providers stop answering. We keep answering until you'd rather we didn't.

The People

Who You'll Actually Meet

🧑‍💼

Your Support Workers

Each house will have two part-time support workers, giving 40 hours of cover across the week. They will hold or be working toward NVQ Level 3 in Health and Social Care, and all will have enhanced DBS clearance.

❤️

Why They Were Hired

We will recruit for warmth and reliability as much as qualifications — for the ability to build trust with people who've had very little reason to trust services before. That part can't be taught.

📋

Paul Sterling, Founding Director

Paul founded The New Start Project and oversees the service, the standards we hold ourselves to, and our relationships with the council and local services — making sure the care we promise is the care you actually get.

Working With Others

Local Services

Some things need specialists. We will connect residents with the local organisations who do them best.

🏛️

Torbay Council

Housing Options, Housing Needs and the leaving care team — for referrals and for making sure people get the housing they're entitled to.

🛡️

FearFree

Domestic abuse specialists providing IDVA support across Torbay.

🌿

TDAS & Recovery Services

Torbay Drug and Alcohol Service and local peer support networks, for anyone working on their recovery.

⚖️

Probation Services

Devon & Cornwall Probation, supporting people leaving prison into stable accommodation.
