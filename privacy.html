Privacy

Privacy Notice

How we collect, use and protect your personal information.

Who we are

The New Start Project Community CIC (company no. 17399292) is responsible for the personal information collected through this website. Contact: contact@thenewstartproject.co.uk or 01803 446133.

What we collect

When you use our contact, landlord or referral forms, we collect the details you give us. This may include names, contact details, property details and, for referrals, information about the person's circumstances, health, substance use and risk.

Why we use it

We use it to reply to your enquiry, to consider a property you offer, or to decide whether we can offer someone a safe place. Our lawful bases are your consent, steps before entering a contract (for landlords), our legitimate interests in responding to enquiries, and, for referral information about health, the provision of social care and support.

Who we share it with

Only New Start staff directly involved see it. Our form service (Web3Forms) and email provider process it on our behalf. We do not sell your information. We only share it with others where you agree, or where the law or keeping someone safe requires it.

How long we keep it

Enquiries that don't go further are deleted within 12 months. Referral and tenancy records are kept only as long as needed for support, legal and safeguarding reasons.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your information, or object to how we use it, by contacting us. If you are unhappy with how we handle your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Last updated: October 2026.
