Where It Goes

Practical help, not overheads first

We are a small South Devon project. Donations are used for the everyday costs that make the difference between surviving and settling.

🛏️

Move-In Essentials

Bedding, towels, kitchen basics and cleaning supplies so a new room is ready on day one.

🚌

Getting to Appointments

Bus fares and travel costs for GP, benefits, probation and housing appointments.

📱

Staying Contactable

Phone credit and data so people can be reached by support workers, employers and services.

🧰

Work and Training

Interview clothes, ID documents, course fees and the kit needed to start a job.

🍲

Food and Warmth

Store cupboard stock and energy costs during the first weeks, before benefits settle.

🔑

Moving On

Deposits, rent in advance and starter packs for people stepping into their own tenancy.

Make a Donation

Give securely in a couple of minutes

Donations are handled through our secure Gumroad checkout. You choose the amount, and card and PayPal payments are both accepted. You will receive an email receipt.

  • One-off donation — give any amount you are comfortable with at checkout.
  • No account needed — you only need an email address and a payment method.
  • Secure by design — we never see or store your card details.
  • Receipted — a confirmation email is sent to you automatically.

Continue to the secure donation page →

The New Start Project CIC is a community interest company, not a registered charity, so donations are not eligible for Gift Aid. If you would like written confirmation of how your donation was used, email contact@thenewstartproject.co.uk.

Our Promise

Dignity first, always

We do not use photographs, names or personal stories of the people we support to raise funds. Nobody has to trade their privacy for a bed, a meal or a bus fare. What we can tell you is what your donation paid for — and we will.
