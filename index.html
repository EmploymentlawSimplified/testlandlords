New Start Project

The New Start Project is a community interest company for people in recovery who are between three months and two years clean. Our approach is simple: ordinary shared houses in South Devon, not hostels, where people can stay clean, learn and grow, and move on to a place of their own.

  • Safe shared homes focused on long-term abstinence
  • One-to-one support built around each person
  • A clear route towards living independently

Landlords: we're looking for three- and four-bedroom houses in South Devon.
