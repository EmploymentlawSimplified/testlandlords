Volunteer With Us

Your time builds something real

Help people in recovery rebuild their lives across South Devon — while gaining training, experience, purpose and connection.

Why Volunteer?

Give back and grow

Whether you want experience in housing and social care, want to support your community, or bring valuable lived experience, there is a place for you here.

🎓

Build Your Career

Gain real-world experience, accredited training, development reviews, references and CV support.

🤝

Make a Difference

Support people through a difficult point in their lives with dignity, patience and practical help.

🌱

Find Purpose

Build structure, connection and meaning as part of a small team that genuinely cares.

Ways to Help

Choose a role that suits you

Hours can be flexible and shaped around your availability, skills and interests.

☕

Conversation and Companionship

Listen, share a cup of tea and help someone feel heard.

🚶

Appointments and Practical Support

Accompany residents to GP, benefits, probation or housing appointments.

🍲

Meals and a Warm Welcome

Prepare food, welcome guests and create a calm, respectful environment.

🛠️

Property and Maintenance

Help with decorating, gardening and preparing rooms.

📝

CV and Employment Support

Help with CVs, forms and interview practice.

🧭

Peer Mentoring

Use relevant lived experience to help someone trust and move forward.

What You Receive

Training, support and a real pathway

  • Funded training including safeguarding, mental health, first aid and role-specific learning.
  • Development opportunities and the possibility of working towards relevant Level 2 or Level 3 qualifications.
  • Regular supervision, mentoring and development reviews.
  • Agreed expenses covered so you are not left out of pocket.
  • Career support through experience, references, testimonials and CV help.
  • Future opportunities to be considered for suitable paid roles as the service grows.

Volunteers are not free labour. Your time should be met with structured training, meaningful experience, proper support and respect.

What We Ask

Care, reliability and willingness to learn

✓

No CV Required

No formal qualification or previous experience is required to start a conversation.

🛡️

Checks and References

We pay for the required DBS check. Personal references can be considered.

💛

Our Values

Be reliable, respectful, willing to learn and able to maintain appropriate boundaries.

Interested? It starts with a conversation.

No formal interview at the first step. Tell us what interests you and what you hope to gain.
